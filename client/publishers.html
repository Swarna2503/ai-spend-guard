For agent ownersDashboard

People read free. AI agents pay.

AI agents read your articles but never see your ads, so you earn nothing from them. Put your articles behind this paywall: human readers pass a quick check and read free, while declared AI agents get a price and pay you through PayPal.

How it works for a publisher

The same flow the three demo sites below use.

1. Set a priceYou choose what an AI agent pays per article. The AI never sets prices.
2. Agents get a 402A declared agent receives an HTTP 402 offer. People get a human check and read free; undeclared bots are challenged.
3. You get paidThe platform verifies the PayPal payment, unlocks the article, and sends the money to your own PayPal account.
